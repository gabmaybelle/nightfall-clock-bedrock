# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first Bedrock release of Nightfall Clock. Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.60 or newer, no experiments.

- The day and time just above your hotbar ("Day 4 · 2:35 PM").
- From 6:00 PM the line turns gold; "The sun's getting real low" when dusk arrives, then a countdown to dark in real seconds.
- "Storm's rolling in" when thunder starts.
- The Overworld's time in the Nether and the End.
- `/nightfall_clock:settings` (every player, no cheats): each part on or off, the line above the hotbar or in a corner, and a 12- or 24-hour clock, saved per player.
